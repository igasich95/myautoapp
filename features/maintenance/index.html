Log it, plan it, get reminded

The whole history of the car stays in one place

  1. 01

    Add the jobs done, what each one cost, the date and the odometer reading.

  2. 02

    Each visit keeps its jobs with prices, the mileage and how far the car went since the visit before.

  3. 03

    Remind by date, by mileage or both — say, every 6 months or 6,000 km. Whichever comes first fires the reminder.

  4. 04

    In the maintenance schedule, oil, filters, brake pads and other parts have their own intervals. MyAuto finds past replacements by the names of the jobs and shows what is left.

What the service book does

  • Work items with prices

    One record can hold several jobs, each with its own cost.

  • Reminders by date or mileage

    A notification before the service is due — whichever limit comes first.

  • Photos of work orders

    Attach photos of receipts and work orders to service records.

  • Maintenance spending

    Statistics show what maintenance costs over any period.

  • PDF report

    A report of the car with maintenance, expenses and fuel-ups — for a buyer or a garage.

  • Kept safe

    iCloud sync and a JSON backup keep the service history when you change phones.

Questions and answers

Are service reminders free?

Yes. Service records, plans and reminders by date or mileage are free. Photos on records, maintenance statistics and the PDF report are part of Pro.

Can I set a reminder by mileage?

Yes — by date, by mileage or both. Whichever comes first fires the reminder, and you choose how far in advance it arrives.

How does MyAuto know my current mileage?

From trips, fill-ups and service records with an odometer reading, or when you enter the mileage yourself.

Can I show the service history to a buyer?

Yes. With Pro, export a PDF report of the car: maintenance with the work done, expenses, fuel-ups and charging, with dates, mileage and amounts.
