Privacy Policy for “My Auto”

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Developer / data controller: Ravil Novruzov (“Developer”, “we”, “us”). Privacy contact: Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the “My Auto” mobile application (the “App”), regardless of their country of residence, unless applicable local law provides otherwise.

If the laws of your country provide additional rights regarding personal data, those rights remain available to you regardless of this Privacy Policy.

2. Privacy summary

The App is designed to help users keep records about vehicles, mileage, trips, expenses, maintenance, fuel fill-ups or charging sessions, and related notes.

The App’s main user records are stored locally on the user’s device. The Developer does not receive, store, or view the vehicle records, receipts, photos, notes, mileage, trips, expenses, fuel or charging records that the user enters into or records with the App, except for the limited data described in section 7.

The App can record trips and use location services for that purpose, including in the background, if the user grants the relevant permission. Trip data, including route coordinates, stays on the user’s device and, where syncing is enabled, in the user’s own iCloud storage. See section 6 for details.

The App includes an optional driver ranking. If the user signs in with Apple and voluntarily joins it, the App sends the Developer’s Ranking server a public profile (nickname, photo, country, vehicle name, trophies) and trip summaries without route coordinates, and other App users see the profile and results. See section 7 for details.

The App uses Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase to collect limited technical, aggregated, and pseudonymous analytics about App usage. This helps the Developer understand how the App is used, improve the interface, detect product issues, and evaluate features such as import/export, statistics, paywall screens, and purchases.

The App does not use Firebase Auth, Firestore, Realtime Database, Remote Config, Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Ads, IDFA/AdSupport, advertising SDKs, or marketing trackers. The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

The App does not sell users’ personal data and does not use it for behavioural advertising or tracking across third-party apps and websites.

3. Data the user may enter into the App

The user may choose to add vehicle-related data to the App, including vehicle name, vehicle photo, fuel type, mileage, trip records, expense records, maintenance records, fuel or charging records, custom categories, and photos of receipts, service documents or other documents.

The content of these records is controlled by the user. Depending on what the user enters or attaches, these records may contain personal information, such as photos, document numbers, addresses, vehicle registration numbers, or other details.

The user may also set up an account profile: a nickname, a profile photo, a country, and the vehicle shown in the driver ranking (see section 7).

4. Data received by the Developer

The Developer does not receive access to the data stored by the user inside the App, including vehicle records, mileage, trips and their route coordinates, expenses, fuel or charging records, photos, documents, custom category names, comments, or notes. The exceptions are the Apple user identifier and email address that the Ranking server receives when the user signs in with Apple, and the public profile and trip summaries without route coordinates that the App sends to that server if the user voluntarily joins the driver ranking (see section 7).

The Developer may receive analytics reports through Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase. These reports may include technical and pseudonymous usage data, such as an app instance identifier, automatically collected app events, and custom events described in this Privacy Policy. These analytics events are designed not to include the user’s local vehicle records or the contents of user files.

The Developer may also receive personal data if the user voluntarily contacts the Developer, for example by email. In that case, the Developer may process the user’s email address, name, message content, and any technical information the user chooses to provide for support purposes.

If the user makes a purchase or subscribes through the App Store, the payment is processed by Apple. The Developer does not receive or store the user’s payment card details. The App may use purchase or subscription status information to unlock paid functionality. The Developer receives this purchase and subscription status information through RevenueCat, as described in section 10, and may send basic purchase-related analytics events to Firebase Analytics as described below.

5. Local storage and backups

The App’s main data is stored locally on the user’s device.

The user may export or import data, for example through a JSON file or PDF report. The user decides where exported files are stored and with whom they are shared.

If iOS backups, iCloud, or other Apple system features are enabled on the user’s device, App data may be included in those backups depending on the user’s device settings and Apple’s rules. The Developer does not control Apple backups and does not have access to them.

Storage of trip data, including route coordinates, is described in section 6.

6. Trip recording and location data

The App can record the user’s trips, including the route travelled, the distance covered, and the start and end time of each trip.

To record trips, the App requests access to location services, including access while the App is in the background (the “Always” location permission in iOS). This permission is requested the first time the user starts recording a trip, not when the App is launched.

Once a trip has started, route recording continues in the background, including while the App is not on screen or the device is locked, and stops when the trip ends.

If the “Auto-record” feature is enabled in the App’s settings, the App detects the start and the end of a trip by itself and records such trips without any separate action by the user. While that feature is enabled, the App accesses the device’s location in the background. If “Auto-record” is turned off, trips are recorded only after the user starts recording manually.

Granting location access is optional. Without this permission the App continues to work, and the user can enter trips and mileage manually.

Location access can be withdrawn or restricted at any time in iOS settings. Trips recorded before the permission is withdrawn remain stored in the App.

Trip data, including route coordinates, is stored locally on the user’s device. If the user has iCloud syncing enabled, that data is stored in the user’s own private iCloud storage. The Developer has no access to that storage.

Route coordinates and other location data from trips are not transmitted to the Developer’s servers and are not disclosed to third parties. If the user joins the driver ranking, only trip summaries without coordinates are sent to the Ranking server (see section 7). Trip data is not used for advertising or tracking and is not shared with data brokers.

Addresses are resolved from coordinates by Apple’s standard services for maps and geocoding. Apple’s handling of those requests is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

The user can delete any trip inside the App. Deleting a trip also deletes the location data associated with it.

Deleting the App removes local trip data. Data that has been synced to iCloud is deleted through iCloud.

Trip data is retained until the user deletes it.

7. Driver ranking

The App includes a driver ranking (the “Ranking”), where participants compare the results of trips recorded in the App with those of other users. Participation in the Ranking is voluntary and is turned off by default. All other App features work without it.

The user takes part in the Ranking only when all of the following conditions are met: the user has signed in with Apple, has an active paid subscription, has a nickname and a vehicle in the App, and has personally turned on the “Join the ranking” option. The user can turn participation off at any time with the same option.

Sign in with Apple is optional. When the user signs in, Apple provides the App with a unique user identifier and, at the first sign-in and only if the user agrees, the user’s name and email address; the user can hide their real email address with Apple’s “Hide My Email” feature. The name, the email address, and the account profile (nickname, profile photo, and Ranking settings) are stored on the device and, if iCloud syncing is enabled, in the user’s private iCloud storage.

When the user signs in with Apple, the App also creates an account for the user on the Ranking server, even if participation is turned off. To do this, the App sends the server a token issued by Apple, from which the server stores the Apple user identifier and, if the token contains it, the email address. The name is not sent to the server.

While participation is turned on, the App sends a public profile to the Ranking server, and other users see it in the Ranking:

  • nickname. Until the user sets their own nickname, the first name from the Apple ID (without the surname) is used; the nickname can be changed at any time;
  • profile photo, if the user has added one. A reduced copy of the photo is uploaded to the server;
  • country: the one chosen by the user or, by default, the device region;
  • vehicle name: as in the App or a separate name the user has set for the Ranking;
  • trophies earned and progress towards those not yet earned;
  • results and places for the period: distance, driving time, longest trip, average speed, and number of trips.

To calculate results, the App also sends the server a summary of each recorded trip from all vehicles in the App: a trip identifier, the start time, the device time zone offset at the start, the distance credited by the Ranking, the time in motion excluding stops and pauses, and whether the distance was corrected manually. Other users do not see individual trips or their start times, only totals for the period and places.

The App does not send the Ranking server route coordinates, addresses, or other location data, and does not send expenses, fuel or charging records, mileage, VIN, licence plate number, photos of vehicles, receipts, or documents, notes, or other vehicle records.

The Ranking, including participants’ public profiles, is visible to all App users, including users who have not signed in. Profile photos can be opened by a direct link without signing in.

The Ranking server runs on the Supabase platform (Supabase, Inc.), which stores the Ranking database, server accounts, and profile photos and runs server functions on the Developer’s instructions. Supabase’s privacy policy is available at: https://supabase.com/privacy. If the device is set to a time zone of Russia, requests to the server may pass through a gateway in the Yandex Cloud service (Russia), which forwards them to Supabase unchanged; the data itself is stored in Supabase. As with any internet service, the server infrastructure processes the device’s IP address and technical request data.

Only participants with an active paid subscription are shown in the Ranking, and the server checks this through RevenueCat. For this purpose, after the user joins the Ranking, the App links the user’s purchases in RevenueCat to the Apple user identifier, as described in section 10.

If participation is turned off or the paid subscription ends, the profile disappears from the Ranking and the App stops sending new data. Data already sent remains on the server, hidden from other users, and appears in the Ranking again if the user rejoins. Signing out of the account in the App does not remove the profile from the Ranking: to leave the Ranking, the user turns off participation (signing in with Apple again if necessary) or deletes the account.

The “Delete account” action in the App deletes the server account, the public profile, profile photos, trip summaries, and subscription status from the Ranking server, deletes the account profile on the device and in iCloud, and revokes Sign in with Apple for the App. If there is no internet connection, deletion on the server is completed the next time the App is launched. Copies of the profile photo may remain in network caches for up to 24 hours.

8. Camera and photos

The App may request access to the camera or photo library only for features chosen by the user, such as attaching a vehicle photo, service document, receipt, or other document, or adding a profile photo.

Photos of vehicles, receipts, and documents are not uploaded to the Developer’s servers or to Firebase Analytics. A reduced copy of the profile photo is uploaded to the Ranking server only if the user joins the driver ranking (see section 7).

The user can manage camera and photo access in iOS settings.

9. Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase

The App uses Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase only for product analytics. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect technical and pseudonymous analytics data, including:

  • a unique app installation identifier, such as an app instance ID;
  • number of users and sessions;
  • session duration;
  • first opens, app opens, and app updates;
  • device model, operating system, App version, and related technical information;
  • device language and locale;
  • approximate geography, such as country or region, derived from technical data such as IP address;
  • basic information about in-app purchases or subscriptions if available through Google Analytics / Firebase.

Approximate geography is not GPS location and is not the user’s precise address.

The App may manually send Firebase Analytics events about actions inside the App, including:

  • completing onboarding;
  • adding a vehicle or switching between vehicles;
  • adding an expense, maintenance record, fuel record, or charging record;
  • attaching a photo to a record;
  • opening statistics or changing a chart type;
  • successful JSON import/export or PDF export;
  • opening a paywall, starting a purchase, successful purchase, monthly or yearly subscription purchase, purchase error, and restore purchases action;
  • comparing results with another Ranking participant and sharing a Ranking result card;
  • a failed request to the Ranking server or a switch of the connection route to it.

The App sends only general, non-personal parameters with these events, such as fuel type (petrol, diesel, or EV), selected App region, whether a photo is attached, record type, category type (default or custom), currency, chart type, paywall source, the number of maintenance items in a maintenance record, the Ranking nomination, period, and scope (world or a country code), the user’s place in the monthly results, and technical details of a failed request to the Ranking server (request name, error code and text, connection route).

The App does not send the following data to Firebase Analytics:

  • VIN;
  • vehicle registration number or license plate;
  • vehicle name;
  • photos of receipts, vehicles, or documents, or the profile photo;
  • the nickname, or the name and email address from the Apple account;
  • contents of PDF or JSON files;
  • user comments, personal notes, or custom category names;
  • precise addresses;
  • route coordinates or other location data from recorded trips;
  • exact expense descriptions;
  • expense amounts, mileage, fuel volume, charging amounts, or other detailed vehicle records.

10. Purchases and subscriptions (RevenueCat)

The App uses the RevenueCat SDK to handle in-app purchases and subscription status. The payment is always processed by Apple through the App Store. Neither the Developer nor RevenueCat receives the user’s payment card details.

The RevenueCat SDK sends the following data to the servers of RevenueCat, Inc. (United States):

  • an anonymous user identifier generated by the SDK itself ($RCAnonymousID);
  • an operating system level device identifier (IDFV);
  • transaction and subscription information from the App Store, such as product identifier, purchase and expiration dates, subscription status, store country, price, and currency;
  • technical information, such as App version, device model, and operating system version.

The App does not send the user’s name or email address to RevenueCat and does not use RevenueCat’s customer attribute or attribution features. If the user does not take part in the driver ranking, the App does not send RevenueCat any account identifier either, and the data listed above is not linked to the user’s identity.

If the user takes part in the driver ranking, the App links the user’s purchases in RevenueCat to the Apple user identifier received through Sign in with Apple, and this identifier becomes the RevenueCat customer identifier, including after the user signs out. The Ranking server then requests the subscription status from RevenueCat by this identifier and receives notifications from RevenueCat about subscription changes. The server stores the RevenueCat customer identifier, the paid subscription expiration date, and whether the purchase was made in a test environment.

RevenueCat, Inc. acts as a data processor on the Developer’s instructions. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is available at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

11. Purposes and lawful bases

For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Developer acts as a controller only for the data that the Developer actually receives or can access, such as support emails, subscription status information, analytics data available in Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase, and data on the Ranking server.

Data is used for the following purposes: providing App functionality; displaying and storing user records locally on the device; recording trips and calculating distance travelled on the device at the user’s request; running the driver ranking for users who have joined it: calculating results and places, showing participants’ public profiles to other users, checking participants’ subscription status, and protecting the Ranking from implausible results; responding to support requests; confirming purchase or subscription status; understanding App usage; improving App design and functionality; measuring feature usage; detecting product issues; evaluating import/export, paywall, and purchase flows; and complying with applicable legal requirements.

Depending on the situation, the lawful basis may be performance of a contract with the user, legitimate interests in supporting, protecting, and improving the App, the user’s consent where required by law, or compliance with legal obligations.

Location access for trip recording, including background access, relies solely on the user’s consent, which is given through the iOS system prompt and can be withdrawn at any time in iOS settings.

Participation in the driver ranking and publication of the public profile rely on the user’s consent, which is given by turning on the “Join the ranking” option and can be withdrawn at any time by turning it off. Creating the server account when the user signs in with Apple and checking the subscription status are necessary to provide the Ranking at the user’s request; checking results for plausibility relies on the Developer’s legitimate interest in keeping the Ranking fair.

12. Sharing data with third parties

The Developer does not share the user’s local App records with third parties because the Developer does not receive access to those records.

Analytics data is processed by Google through Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase. Google’s processing of analytics data is governed by Google’s and Firebase’s applicable terms and policies.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The user’s relationship with Apple is governed by Apple’s terms and policies.

Addresses are resolved from trip coordinates by Apple’s standard services for maps and geocoding, subject to Apple’s privacy policy. The recorded routes and trip coordinates themselves are not disclosed to the Developer, Google, RevenueCat, Supabase, or any other third party.

Data about purchase and subscription status is additionally processed by RevenueCat, Inc. on the Developer’s instructions, as described in section 10.

Ranking data is stored and processed by Supabase, Inc. on the Developer’s instructions. Requests from devices set to a time zone of Russia may pass through the Yandex Cloud service, as described in section 7. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

The public profile and results of a Ranking participant are visible to other App users. The Developer does not otherwise disclose Ranking data to third parties.

If the user contacts the Developer by email, the message may be processed by email service providers used to deliver and store email.

The App does not use third-party advertising networks, AdMob, Google Ads, or SDKs for tracking users across third-party apps and websites.

13. International data transfers

Because the App’s main user records are stored locally on the device, the Developer does not transfer those local records internationally. The same applies to trip data, including route coordinates.

Ranking data is stored on the servers of Supabase, Inc., which may be located outside the user’s country of residence. Requests from devices set to a time zone of Russia may pass through Yandex Cloud servers in Russia.

Analytics data processed through Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase may be processed by Google in different countries according to Google’s terms, policies, and applicable law.

Analytics data and data about purchase status may be transferred to and stored on the servers of Google and RevenueCat, Inc. in the United States and other countries.

If the user contacts support by email or makes a purchase through the App Store, relevant data may be processed by Apple, email providers, Google, or other infrastructure providers in different countries according to their own policies and applicable law.

14. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the user’s device until the user deletes individual records, deletes the App’s data, or deletes the App from the device.

Trip data, including route coordinates, is retained on the same terms: it is removed when the user deletes the trip, the App’s data, or the App itself. Data that has been synced to iCloud is deleted through iCloud.

The Developer cannot remotely delete or modify the user’s local data because the Developer does not have access to it.

Ranking data on the server (the server account, public profile, profile photo, trip summaries, and subscription status) is retained until the user deletes the account in the App or asks the Developer to delete it. Turning off participation hides this data from other users but does not delete it. A trip deleted in the App is also deleted from the server the next time the App sends Ranking data while participation is turned on.

Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of the relevant Google Analytics property and Google’s applicable policies. The actual retention period should be checked in the Google Analytics property settings.

Support messages may be retained for as long as necessary to respond to the user, resolve an issue, protect the Developer’s legitimate interests, or comply with legal obligations.

Purchase and subscription information is stored and processed by Apple according to Apple’s rules.

To request deletion of subscription data processed by RevenueCat, the user sends a request to the Developer by email at Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru. The Developer then instructs RevenueCat to delete the record, because RevenueCat acts as a processor only on the Developer’s instructions.

15. User rights

The user can view, change, export, or delete local records, including trip records, inside the App where the relevant feature is available, or delete the App from the device.

Inside the App, the user can change the public profile, turn off participation in the driver ranking, or delete the account together with all Ranking data on the server. The user can also request a copy or deletion of Ranking data by email.

Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom may have the right to request access to personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing, and withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

Because the Developer does not receive local App data, the Developer may be technically unable to provide a copy, correct, or delete data that exists only on the user’s device.

A request to delete subscription data processed by RevenueCat is sent to the Developer by email, after which the Developer instructs RevenueCat to delete the record, because RevenueCat acts as a processor only on the Developer’s instructions.

To exercise data rights or ask privacy questions, contact us at Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru. Users may also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

16. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. In countries where a higher age of consent is required for processing children’s data, the App is not intended to be used by children without the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

The Developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data to the Developer, please contact us at Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru.

17. Security

The Developer takes reasonable steps to design the App with local data storage and data minimization in mind.

Access to the Ranking database is restricted: each user can change only their own data, and other users receive only public profiles and totals for the period.

The security of local data also depends on the user’s device, iOS settings, device passcode, backups, and the user’s own actions.

Users are encouraged to protect their devices with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID and to be careful when sharing exported files or photos of documents with third parties.
