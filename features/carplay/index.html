Everything you need while driving, without opening the app

What the Live Activity does

  • CarPlay card

    The current trip’s distance and time on the car’s screen, next to maps and music.

  • Lock Screen controls

    Start, pause, resume and finish the trip without opening the app.

  • Dynamic Island

    The trip stays in view while you use other apps, with the same controls.

  • Choose the car

    Pick which car the trip belongs to right from the Live Activity.

  • Add a fill-up

    Jump straight to a new fill-up from the active trip.

What’s free and what’s Pro

Pro is monthly, yearly or Lifetime; the price is shown in the App Store before you pay. Compare Free and Pro

Requirements and limits

  • MyAuto isn’t a CarPlay app: on the car’s screen it appears as a Live Activity card.
  • The card is turned on in the car: CarPlay → Settings → Live Activity.
  • The CarPlay card shows distance and time; the buttons are on the Lock Screen and in Dynamic Island.
  • MyAuto is made for iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later.

Questions and answers

Is MyAuto a CarPlay app?

No. MyAuto shows the current trip on the CarPlay screen as a Live Activity card with its distance and time.

How do I show the trip on CarPlay?

Start a trip, then on the car’s screen open CarPlay settings and turn on Live Activity.

Can I control the trip from the Lock Screen?

Yes. Start, pause, resume and finish from the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island, pick the car or jump to a new fill-up.

Can a trip start by itself when I connect to CarPlay?

Yes, with a Shortcuts automation: start the trip when CarPlay connects, finish it when CarPlay disconnects. MyAuto shows the steps in its settings.
