MyAuto features

Expenses, fuel, service and trips for your car — with the numbers worked out for you. Here is everything the app does, and what is free and what comes with Pro

Get MyAuto — free Compare Free and Pro

Nine things MyAuto does

Each page explains how the feature works, what it needs and what it costs

  • Expenses

    Repairs, car washes, parking, insurance — spending by ready-made or your own categories.

    Learn more

  • Fuel and charging

    Petrol, diesel, gas or electricity — and two fuels in one entry for cars on gas.

    Learn more

  • Service and reminders

    Service records with work items, and reminders by date or mileage.

    Learn more

  • A garage for up to 10 cars

    Each car with its own VIN, photo, mileage and history. One car is free.

    Learn more

  • Joining the ranking

    Your place on the boards for your country and the world, and 12 trophies for trips. Viewing is free.

    Learn more

  • Trip recording

    Route, distance, time and speed of every drive. Recording can start on its own, and the mileage updates itself.

    Learn more

  • Statistics and reports

    Cost of ownership, cost per km or mile, fuel consumption, a month in review and a PDF report of the car.

    Learn more

  • CarPlay and Live Activity

    The current trip on CarPlay, the Lock Screen and in Dynamic Island.

    Learn more

  • Car data via OBD

    Mileage, tire pressure, battery, fuel and engine data through a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi OBD II adapter.

    Learn more

Built for your country

14 regional presets set the currency, units and local fuel station brands.
Anywhere else, choose Other region. The interface comes in 10 languages
