Three ways to get Pro

Choose one on the Pro screen in the app. Apple handles the payment

  • Monthly

    A subscription that renews every month until you cancel it.

  • Yearly

    A subscription that renews every year until you cancel it.

  • Lifetime

    A one-time purchase: Pro stays yours, with no renewals or charges.

The price depends on your country and is shown in the App Store before you pay.

Good to know

  • Payment goes through Apple. Neither MyAuto nor RevenueCat, which keeps the subscription status, ever sees your card details.
  • Cancel a subscription any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. In MyAuto, “Cancel subscription” on the Pro screen opens the same settings.
  • On a new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap “Restore purchases” on the Pro screen.
  • If Pro ends, nothing is deleted. The first car in your garage stays fully available, and the other cars wait, locked, until Pro is back. Trip recording, statistics and photos on records lock again, and your profile leaves the driver ranking.

Questions about Free and Pro

Is MyAuto free?

Yes. One car with its expenses, fuel-ups and charging, service records and reminders is free, with no time limit. Pro adds trip recording, statistics — fuel consumption and the cost of ownership — a PDF report, photos on service and expense records and up to 10 cars.

Is there a one-time purchase?

Yes, Lifetime: you pay once and keep Pro with no renewals. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available too.

How do I cancel Pro?

In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MyAuto, or with “Cancel subscription” on the Pro screen in the app. Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Will Pro carry over to my new iPhone?

Yes, with the same Apple Account: open the Pro screen in MyAuto and tap “Restore purchases”.

What happens to my records if Pro ends?

They stay. The first car in your garage remains fully available; the other cars and the Pro features lock until you get Pro again.

Why isn’t the price on this page?

It differs from country to country. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you pay.
