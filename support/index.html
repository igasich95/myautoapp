Purchases and refunds

  • Payments go through Apple, so a refund is requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Pro missing after reinstalling or on a new iPhone? Open the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases” with the same Apple Account.
  • To cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Questions about support

How do I contact MyAuto?

Write to Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru or use the Telegram channel. In the app, both are in Profile → Feedback.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Pro is gone after I reinstalled the app. What do I do?

Open the Pro screen in MyAuto and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I ask to delete my data?

Most data is deleted right in the app: records, iCloud data and the account. For subscription data kept by RevenueCat or a copy of your ranking data, write to Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru.
