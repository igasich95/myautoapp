Log the fill-up. Get the numbers

Consumption, spending and prices come from the entries you already make

  1. 01

    Petrol, diesel, gas, hybrid or electric. Your region sets the units and the petrol grades.

  2. 02

    The station, the fuel, the price per litre or the total, the volume, the date and the odometer. One fill-up can hold two fuels — say, propane and petrol.

  3. 03

    Each fill-up shows the consumption per 100 km or per 100 miles, how the price changed and the distance since the previous fill-up (Pro).

  4. 04

    Fuel spending by month, the litres and your favourite station (Pro).

What the fuel log does

  • Every fuel type

    Petrol grades for your region, diesel, methane, propane, hybrid and electric.

  • Consumption, worked out

    Per 100 km or per 100 miles — for fuel and for energy.

  • Your fuel stations

    The station brands of your country are already in the list; the statistics show your favourite station.

  • Two fuels in one fill-up

    For cars on gas: propane or methane and petrol in the same entry.

  • Spending and prices

    Average fuel spending and how the price you pay changes over time.

  • From the active trip

    Add a fill-up right from the trip’s Live Activity on the Lock Screen.

Requirements and limits

  • Consumption is most accurate when every fill-up has an odometer reading — MyAuto points out entries without one.
  • Methane is counted in cubic metres; electric cars log charging in kWh.
  • Without Pro the statistics inside a fill-up are hidden.
  • MyAuto is made for iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later.

Questions and answers

How does MyAuto work out fuel consumption?

From the volume and the odometer readings of your fill-ups. Keep the odometer in every entry for accurate numbers; the result is shown per 100 km or per 100 miles. Consumption statistics are part of Pro.

Does it work for cars on LPG or CNG?

Yes. Choose gas — propane or methane — as the fuel. One fill-up can hold gas and petrol together, and methane is counted in cubic metres.

Can I track EV charging?

Yes. Choose electric and log each charge in kWh with its price. A hybrid can log both fuel and charging.

Is the fuel log free?

Logging fill-ups and charging is free. Consumption, fuel spending and price statistics are part of Pro.

Which units can I use?

Litres, gallons, cubic metres or kWh, and kilometres or miles. A regional preset picks them for you; under Other region you set them yourself.
