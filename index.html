Questions and answers

Is MyAuto free?

Yes. One car with its expenses, fuel-ups and charging, service records and reminders is free. Pro adds trip recording, statistics — fuel consumption and the cost of ownership — a PDF report, photos on service and expense records and up to 10 cars.

Does MyAuto record trips automatically?

Trip recording is part of Pro. You can start a trip in the app or from the Lock Screen. For hands-free recording, create two Shortcuts automations once: one starts the trip when your iPhone connects to CarPlay or your car’s Bluetooth, the other finishes it when it disconnects. With Record automatically on, recording begins once the car gets moving.

Does MyAuto work with CarPlay?

MyAuto isn’t a separate CarPlay app. While a trip is recording, its Live Activity with the distance and time appears on the CarPlay screen. Turn it on in CarPlay in your car: Settings → Live Activity → On.

Which fuels and units are supported?

Petrol (grades depend on your region), diesel, methane or propane, hybrid and electric. Volume in litres, gallons or cubic metres, energy in kWh, distance in kilometres or miles, and 17 currencies.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you sign in with Apple and turn on sync, it’s also kept in your own private iCloud, which the developer can’t access. Trip routes are never sent to MyAuto’s servers.

Is there an Android version?

No. MyAuto is made for iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later.

Which languages is MyAuto available in?

Ten: English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Dutch, Polish and Turkish.
