Car cost statistics and PDF report

MyAuto turns your records into numbers: the cost of ownership, the cost of each kilometre or mile, the monthly cost, fuel use and spending by category for any period — and exports it all as a PDF report. Statistics and reports are part of Pro

What the statistics show

  • Cost of ownership

    Everything the car has cost you, shown next to its purchase price.

  • Cost per 1 km or 1 mi

    What each kilometre or mile really costs.

  • Monthly cost

    How much the car costs per month.

  • Fuel and energy use

    Per 100 km or per 100 miles, plus your favourite station.

  • Mileage history

    How the mileage grows, from trips, fill-ups, service and manual entries.

  • PDF report

    Totals and every record, ready to send to a buyer or a garage.

What’s free and what’s Pro

Statistics and reports are part of Pro. Without Pro you still log expenses, fill-ups and service for free — the values are simply hidden.

  • Cost of ownership, cost per km or mile, monthly cost
  • Fuel and energy use, favourite station
  • Spending by category, comparisons with last year or the average
  • Month in review and share cards
  • Mileage history
  • PDF report of the car

Pro is monthly, yearly or Lifetime; the price is shown in the App Store before you pay. Compare Free and Pro

Requirements and limits

  • All-time statistics appear once a car has at least 10 records.
  • Odometer readings in your records make the numbers more accurate — MyAuto shows where they are missing.
  • Without Pro the values are hidden.
  • MyAuto is made for iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later.

Questions and answers

What does the PDF report include?

A mileage and expenses log for one car: expenses, maintenance with the work done, fuel-ups and charging, each with date, mileage and amount, plus the total spent.

Can I compare periods?

Yes. Compare any period with the same period last year or with your all-time average.

What is the month in review?

A recap of your spending, mileage and fuel-ups for the month on one screen. You can share it on social media as a card with your spending or your trips.

Are statistics free?

No, statistics and the PDF report are part of Pro. Logging the records they are built from is free.
