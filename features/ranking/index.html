Driver ranking and trophies

MyAuto’s ranking compares your recorded trips with drivers in your country and around the world — by distance, number of trips, time on the road, record trip or average speed — for today, the week or the month. Your trips also earn 12 trophies. Viewing the ranking is free; joining it needs Pro and Sign in with Apple

Drive, then see where you stand

Recorded trips count on their own

  1. 01

    Sign in with Apple, choose a nickname and a photo, and turn on participation. Your place then shows on your avatar.

  2. 02

    Every trip you record in MyAuto counts towards the boards and the trophies.

  3. 03

    Pick the board, the period and the metric — and tap any participant to compare your results.

What the ranking does

  • Boards

    Worldwide or your country.

  • Five metrics

    Distance, trips, time on the road, record trip and average speed.

  • Periods

    Today, this week or this month, plus monthly results.

  • 12 trophies

    From your first trip to No borders — start and finish a trip in different countries.

  • Cards to share

    A “My trophies” postcard and a card with your ranking results.

  • Privacy

    Only a public profile and trip totals — no routes — go to the ranking server.

Requirements and limits

  • Joining needs Pro, Sign in with Apple and a nickname.
  • Only trips recorded in MyAuto count.
  • You can turn participation off or delete the account with all ranking data right in the app.
  • Always keep to the speed limit and the rules of the road.

Questions and answers

Is the ranking free?

Viewing the boards is free. Joining needs Pro and Sign in with Apple, because the ranking is built from recorded trips.

What data goes to the ranking?

A public profile — nickname, photo, country, car name and trophies — and trip totals without route coordinates. Other participants see the profile and the results.

Which trophies are there?

Twelve, for example: First trip; Insomnia — a trip between midnight and 5 a.m.; No days off — trips 7 days in a row; Familiar route — 10 trips on the same route; All year round — trips in winter, spring, summer and autumn; No borders — start and finish a trip in different countries.

Can I leave the ranking?

Yes. Turn participation off at any time, or delete the account together with all ranking data on the server right in the app.
