Where MyAuto keeps your data

On your iPhone. Cars, records, photos and trip routes stay on the device and sync only to your own iCloud, if you turn that on. Here is what stays, what leaves the device and when, and how to delete it

Get MyAuto — free Privacy Policy

Updated: November 22, 2026

What stays on your iPhone

The developer has no access to what you keep in MyAuto

  • Cars and records

    Cars, expenses, fuel-ups and charging, service records, mileage, notes and photos are stored on your device. The developer doesn’t receive them.

    Privacy Policy, section 4

  • Trips and routes

    Routes, distances and times stay on the device. Route coordinates are never sent to the developer or to third parties.

    Privacy Policy, section 6

  • Car data from OBD

    The VIN and the readings from an OBD adapter are processed on the iPhone and aren’t sent to the developer, to analytics or to anyone else.

    Privacy Policy, section 8

  • Motion data

    If you allow Motion & Fitness, MyAuto uses it only to notice that a trip has started. Readings aren’t stored or sent anywhere.

    Privacy Policy, section 6

Your own iCloud — only if you want it

Sync between your devices without giving anyone else access

  • Private iCloud sync

    With sync turned on, your data, trips included, is kept in your private iCloud storage. The developer has no access to it.

    Privacy Policy, section 6

  • Files you control

    Save a car to a JSON file or export a PDF report. You decide where the file goes and who gets it.

    Privacy Policy, section 5

What leaves the device, and why

Only what a feature needs to work. Never your records or routes

  • Usage analytics

    Firebase Analytics receives pseudonymous app events, such as “a fuel-up was added”. Not amounts, mileage, notes, photos, the VIN, the car name or locations.

    Privacy Policy, section 10

  • Purchases

    Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat keeps the subscription status. Nobody but Apple sees your card details.

    Privacy Policy, section 11

  • Sign in with Apple

    Signing in creates an account on the ranking server with your Apple user identifier and, if Apple passes it on, your email — even if you never join the ranking.

    Privacy Policy, section 7

  • Driver ranking, if you join

    Join it with Pro, and your nickname, photo, country, car name, trophies and trip totals go to the ranking server, where others see them. Routes never do.

    Privacy Policy, section 7

  • Car model file for OBD

    To read a car correctly, MyAuto downloads a description of its model from the open OBDb database. The request contains only the model name.

    Privacy Policy, section 8

  • Addresses on the map

    Apple’s maps services turn trip coordinates into addresses, under Apple’s privacy policy.

    Privacy Policy, section 6

What MyAuto doesn’t do

  • No ads, no advertising SDKs and no tracking across other apps and websites. MyAuto doesn’t ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Your data is never sold.
  • Photos of cars, receipts and documents aren’t uploaded anywhere. Only a reduced copy of your profile photo goes to the ranking server, and only if you join the ranking.
  • No account is required: Sign in with Apple is optional.

Permissions

Each one is optional and asked only when a feature needs it. Without it the rest of the app keeps working, and you can withdraw it any time in iOS Settings

Location, “Always”
Records trips in the background. Asked when you start your first trip.
Motion & Fitness
Notices the start of a trip when location is unreliable. Asked when you turn on automatic recording.
Bluetooth and Local Network
Connect to an OBD adapter. Asked when you first search for or connect to one.
Camera and Photos
Attach photos to a car, a record or your profile.

How to delete your data

  • Delete any record or trip in the app. A trip’s location data goes with it.
  • “Erase iCloud data” turns sync off and removes the cloud backup. Cars and records stay on this iPhone.
  • “Delete account” deletes your photo, name and ranking results from the device, iCloud and the ranking server and revokes Sign in with Apple. Cars and records stay.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the iPhone. Data synced to iCloud is deleted through iCloud.
  • To delete subscription data kept by RevenueCat, or to get a copy of your ranking data, write to Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru.

Questions about your data

Can the developer see my records?

No. Records, photos, mileage and trips are stored on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your private iCloud. The developer has no access to either.

Are my trip routes uploaded anywhere?

No. Route coordinates stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Even the driver ranking receives only trip totals, without routes.

Do I need an account to use MyAuto?

No. Sign in with Apple only if you want iCloud sync, a profile or the driver ranking.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud sync, or save each car to a JSON file in Profile → Backups and load it on the new iPhone.

Does MyAuto show ads or sell data?

No. There are no ads or advertising SDKs, and your data is never sold.
