General

Is MyAuto free?

Yes. One car with its expenses, fuel-ups and charging, service records and reminders is free. Pro adds trip recording, statistics — fuel consumption and the cost of ownership — a PDF report, photos on service and expense records and up to 10 cars.

Does MyAuto record trips automatically?

Trip recording is part of Pro. You can start a trip in the app or from the Lock Screen. For hands-free recording, create two Shortcuts automations once: one starts the trip when your iPhone connects to CarPlay or your car’s Bluetooth, the other finishes it when it disconnects. With Record automatically on, recording begins once the car gets moving.

Does MyAuto work with CarPlay?

MyAuto isn’t a separate CarPlay app. While a trip is recording, its Live Activity with the distance and time appears on the CarPlay screen. Turn it on in CarPlay in your car: Settings → Live Activity → On.

Which fuels and units are supported?

Petrol (grades depend on your region), diesel, methane or propane, hybrid and electric. Volume in litres, gallons or cubic metres, energy in kWh, distance in kilometres or miles, and 17 currencies.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you sign in with Apple and turn on sync, it’s also kept in your own private iCloud, which the developer can’t access. Trip routes are never sent to MyAuto’s servers.

Is there an Android version?

No. MyAuto is made for iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later.

Which languages is MyAuto available in?

Ten: English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Dutch, Polish and Turkish.

Free and Pro

More on this
Is there a one-time purchase?

Yes, Lifetime: you pay once and keep Pro with no renewals. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available too.

How do I cancel Pro?

In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MyAuto, or with “Cancel subscription” on the Pro screen in the app. Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Will Pro carry over to my new iPhone?

Yes, with the same Apple Account: open the Pro screen in MyAuto and tap “Restore purchases”.

What happens to my records if Pro ends?

They stay. The first car in your garage remains fully available; the other cars and the Pro features lock until you get Pro again.

Why isn’t the price on this page?

It differs from country to country. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you pay.

Expenses

More on this
Is the car expense tracker free?

Yes. Logging expenses for one car is free — categories, purchase and sale, and leaving one-offs out of the totals. The statistics — cost of ownership, cost per km or mile, monthly cost — are part of Pro.

Which expense categories are there?

Repairs, car washes, parking, insurance, tuning and tires and wheels are ready to use, plus the purchase and sale of the car. You can add your own categories too.

Can I track expenses for several cars?

Yes. One car is free; with Pro the garage holds up to 10 cars, each with its own expenses and statistics.

Can I leave an expense out of the statistics?

Yes — mark it as excluded from totals. It stays in the history but doesn’t change the totals.

Can I export my expenses?

With Pro, a PDF report of the car lists its expenses, maintenance, fuel-ups and charging with dates, mileage and amounts. A JSON backup of the car is free.

Fuel and charging

More on this
How does MyAuto work out fuel consumption?

From the volume and the odometer readings of your fill-ups. Keep the odometer in every entry for accurate numbers; the result is shown per 100 km or per 100 miles. Consumption statistics are part of Pro.

Does it work for cars on LPG or CNG?

Yes. Choose gas — propane or methane — as the fuel. One fill-up can hold gas and petrol together, and methane is counted in cubic metres.

Can I track EV charging?

Yes. Choose electric and log each charge in kWh with its price. A hybrid can log both fuel and charging.

Is the fuel log free?

Logging fill-ups and charging is free. Consumption, fuel spending and price statistics are part of Pro.

Which units can I use?

Litres, gallons, cubic metres or kWh, and kilometres or miles. A regional preset picks them for you; under Other region you set them yourself.

Trips

More on this
Where are my routes stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on sync. Route coordinates are never sent to MyAuto’s servers — even the ranking gets only trip totals.

Why does the distance differ from my odometer?

GPS usually reads a little short. You can enter the distance from the odometer, and the mileage entry follows.

Is trip recording free?

No, trip recording is part of Pro, together with the Live Activity and the CarPlay card.

Service and reminders

More on this
Are service reminders free?

Yes. Service records, plans and reminders by date or mileage are free. Photos on records, maintenance statistics and the PDF report are part of Pro.

Can I set a reminder by mileage?

Yes — by date, by mileage or both. Whichever comes first fires the reminder, and you choose how far in advance it arrives.

How does MyAuto know my current mileage?

From trips, fill-ups and service records with an odometer reading, or when you enter the mileage yourself.

Can I show the service history to a buyer?

Yes. With Pro, export a PDF report of the car: maintenance with the work done, expenses, fuel-ups and charging, with dates, mileage and amounts.

Statistics and reports

More on this
What does the PDF report include?

A mileage and expenses log for one car: expenses, maintenance with the work done, fuel-ups and charging, each with date, mileage and amount, plus the total spent.

Can I compare periods?

Yes. Compare any period with the same period last year or with your all-time average.

What is the month in review?

A recap of your spending, mileage and fuel-ups for the month on one screen. You can share it on social media as a card with your spending or your trips.

Are statistics free?

No, statistics and the PDF report are part of Pro. Logging the records they are built from is free.

CarPlay and Live Activity

More on this
Is MyAuto a CarPlay app?

No. MyAuto shows the current trip on the CarPlay screen as a Live Activity card with its distance and time.

How do I show the trip on CarPlay?

Start a trip, then on the car’s screen open CarPlay settings and turn on Live Activity.

Can I control the trip from the Lock Screen?

Yes. Start, pause, resume and finish from the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island, pick the car or jump to a new fill-up.

Can a trip start by itself when I connect to CarPlay?

Yes, with a Shortcuts automation: start the trip when CarPlay connects, finish it when CarPlay disconnects. MyAuto shows the steps in its settings.

Car data via OBD

More on this
Which OBD adapter do I need?

An OBD II adapter with Bluetooth LE, such as vLinker BM+ or MC+, Veepeak BLE or OBDLink CX, or one with Wi-Fi. Adapters with classic Bluetooth — vLinker FS or MS and cheap ELM327 v1.5 — don’t work with iPhone.

Does a Wi-Fi adapter work with CarPlay?

Wired CarPlay works as usual. Wireless CarPlay may not work while iPhone is connected to the adapter’s network: iPhone stays on one Wi-Fi network at a time, and wireless CarPlay runs over the car’s Wi-Fi.

Which cars does it work with?

Cars with an OBD II port. Speed, RPM, engine temperature and fuel are standard data almost every car reports; tire pressure, the exact mileage and the battery charge are the maker’s own signals and not every model has them. MyAuto recognises the car by its VIN and picks the signal descriptions from the open OBDb database.

Do I have to connect the adapter every time?

No. With Auto-connect on, MyAuto connects to the last adapter by itself: a Bluetooth one even in the background, a Wi-Fi one while the app is open or recording a trip. If you swipe the app away, connect it by hand once.

Where does the car’s data go?

The readings are stored on your iPhone; the mileage goes into the mileage history and syncs through your own iCloud if sync is on. The model name is sent out to download its signal file. After a check you can tap “Send a report” to send the developer an anonymous compatibility report, without your account, trips or full VIN. It is never sent on its own.

Is it free?

The compatibility check is free and works before you subscribe: tap “Check my car”, and MyAuto shows which blocks your car provides. Reading car data day to day is part of Pro, which is monthly, yearly or Lifetime. Expenses, fill-ups and service for one car stay free.

Garage

More on this
How many cars can I add?

One car is free. With Pro the garage holds up to 10 cars, each with its own expenses, fill-ups, service, trips and statistics.

What happens to my cars if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. The extra cars are locked and come back when you renew Pro.

Can I move a car to a new iPhone?

Yes. Sign in with Apple and turn on iCloud sync, or save a JSON backup of the car and import it on the new phone.

Can I change the app icon to my car?

Yes, for free: there are 36 alternate app icons across 12 car brands.

Ranking and trophies

More on this
Is the ranking free?

Viewing the boards is free. Joining needs Pro and Sign in with Apple, because the ranking is built from recorded trips.

What data goes to the ranking?

A public profile — nickname, photo, country, car name and trophies — and trip totals without route coordinates. Other participants see the profile and the results.

Which trophies are there?

Twelve, for example: First trip; Insomnia — a trip between midnight and 5 a.m.; No days off — trips 7 days in a row; Familiar route — 10 trips on the same route; All year round — trips in winter, spring, summer and autumn; No borders — start and finish a trip in different countries.

Can I leave the ranking?

Yes. Turn participation off at any time, or delete the account together with all ranking data on the server right in the app.

Your data

More on this
Can the developer see my records?

No. Records, photos, mileage and trips are stored on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your private iCloud. The developer has no access to either.

Are my trip routes uploaded anywhere?

No. Route coordinates stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Even the driver ranking receives only trip totals, without routes.

Do I need an account to use MyAuto?

No. Sign in with Apple only if you want iCloud sync, a profile or the driver ranking.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud sync, or save each car to a JSON file in Profile → Backups and load it on the new iPhone.

Does MyAuto show ads or sell data?

No. There are no ads or advertising SDKs, and your data is never sold.

Support

More on this
How do I contact MyAuto?

Write to Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru or use the Telegram channel. In the app, both are in Profile → Feedback.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Pro is gone after I reinstalled the app. What do I do?

Open the Pro screen in MyAuto and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I ask to delete my data?

Most data is deleted right in the app: records, iCloud data and the account. For subscription data kept by RevenueCat or a copy of your ranking data, write to Myautoapp.iOS@yandex.ru.
