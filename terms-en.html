The App includes a driver ranking (the “Ranking”), where participants compare the results of trips recorded in the App, such as distance, driving time, longest trip, average speed, and number of trips, and see their places among other participants for different periods, worldwide or within a country.

Participation in the Ranking is voluntary and turned off by default. To take part, you need to sign in with Apple, have an active paid subscription, set a nickname, add a vehicle, and turn on the “Join the ranking” option. You can turn participation off at any time with the same option. If your subscription ends, your profile is no longer shown in the Ranking.

By joining the Ranking, you agree that your nickname, profile photo, country, vehicle name, trophies, results, and places will be shown to other App users, including users who have not signed in. Results are calculated automatically and may contain errors, including because of the trip recording accuracy limits described in section 6. To keep the Ranking fair, a manually corrected trip distance is counted only up to a plausible limit, and trips with an implausible average speed are not counted. The Developer may check, correct, or exclude results that look implausible.

The Developer may change the Ranking rules, nominations, periods, and calculation methods, and may suspend or discontinue the Ranking. The Ranking requires an internet connection.

Signing out of your account in the App does not remove your profile from the Ranking. To leave the Ranking, turn off participation or delete your account in the App; deleting your account removes your profile, photo, and results from the Ranking server. How Ranking data is processed is described in the Privacy Policy.
